Features

DAT - Source of Truth

Load a DAT file from No-Intro, TOSEC, Redump, etc. and scan your ROMs against it. This is the industry standard for verifying your set is complete and accurate.

Remove Broken Files

Scans your ROMs and remove any files that do not match the DAT. This includes files that are corrupt and likely to be from bad dumps.

Find Missing Games

ROM sets you find online are almost always missing games. RetroCheck will find and list all the games that are missing from your set in a text file.

Apply Custom Filters

RetroCheck comes with several custom filters for further cleaning your set. This is useful if you want one game per region etc.

Scrape Artwork

RetroCheck allows you to scrape artwork from the largest online database for retro games. This includes box art, screenshots, videos, manuals and more.

Modern Design

Welcome to a modern, clean design that is easy to use and navigate. Current tools are dated and hard to use. RetroCheck is designed to be the modern & easy to use.

Cross-Platform

Current tools are almost always Windows exclusive. RetroCheck is a cross-platform app that works on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Completely Private

RetroCheck does not collect any data from you. All your data is stored locally on your machine. No data is sent to any server.
